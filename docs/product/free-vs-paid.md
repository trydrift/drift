# Free vs Paid

Correctness is never paywalled. Drift must not make a less accurate breaking
change decision, hide a gap, or weaken verification because a repository is on a
free tier.

## Free / Open Core

- Unlimited local repositories.
- All deterministic analysis and evidence.
- All confidence and gap reporting.
- Local plan DAG.
- Local verification.
- BYO agent/model.
- Report export.
- Benchmark runner.
- Free public-repository hosted analysis, subject to abuse limits.

## Team Paid

- Managed private-repository GitHub/GitLab app.
- Scheduled analysis.
- Persistent evidence cache.
- Dashboards.
- Multi-repo campaigns.
- Shared migration memory.
- Policies.
- Hosted sandbox execution.
- Collaboration integrations.
- Analytics.
- Standard support.

## Enterprise

- SSO, SCIM, and RBAC.
- Audit.
- Self-host, VPC, and data residency.
- Private registries and internal packages.
- Custom analyzers.
- Model policy.
- Compliance controls.
- SLA.
- Managed modernization.

Paid tiers buy operations, scale, collaboration, governance, hosted isolation,
support, and persistence. They do not buy a more truthful answer.
